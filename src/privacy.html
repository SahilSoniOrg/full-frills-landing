Privacy Policy for Full Frills Balance

Effective date: October 16, 2026 · Last updated: October 16, 2026

The short version

  • Your financial records are stored on your device. We don't store them on our servers.
  • No bank logins, no account sign-up, and no online sync or cloud backup in the current app.
  • SMS import is optional, Android-only, and parsed on your device. Transaction SMS you capture are kept on your phone until you clear app data, reset, or uninstall.
  • We do not intentionally send amounts, balances, merchant names, SMS text or your notes in analytics or crash reports. Analytics is on in release builds and there is currently no in-app switch to turn it off.
  • Voice entry is optional. Your phone's speech-recognition service turns speech into text and, depending on your phone, may process the audio online.
  • If Android device backup is on, Android may back up the app's data to your Google account.
  • Backup files you export are not encrypted by the app and can include original SMS text, so keep them somewhere safe.

1. Overview

Full Frills Balance is designed primarily as an offline personal money management application. It does not connect to bank accounts; financial records are based on information you enter, SMS messages you choose to import, or files you choose to import.

Your financial records are stored on your device and are not uploaded to our servers. A small amount of data does leave your device for the purposes described in this policy: allowlisted analytics and crash reports (section 5), voice entry through your phone's speech service (section 4), exchange-rate and update-check requests (section 6), and Android device backup if you have it turned on (section 7). Section 9 lists everyone who may receive data.

2. Financial Data Stored Locally

The following data is stored on your device:

  • income and expense entries, including planned (future-dated) entries
  • balances
  • journals
  • account names
  • categories and budgets
  • transaction descriptions
  • imported transaction records, including retained transaction SMS (see section 3)
  • rule-based tagging preferences

We do not store this financial data on our own servers.

The app may also store the version of this Privacy Policy you acknowledged and the acknowledgement time. That local record is the app's acknowledgement state and remains on your device. If product analytics is enabled, the app may send a best-effort pseudonymous acknowledgement event to PostHog with the policy version as its event-specific data, alongside the standard analytics and device context described below. This telemetry is not a signed legal record. It does not send the policy text, a signature, or your financial records.

3. SMS Access and Transaction Import

On Android, Full Frills Balance may request SMS permissions to check messages for transaction details. Automatic SMS import is optional and starts only after you enable it in Settings.

SMS access is used only to:

  • scan your inbox when you manually start an import
  • read incoming SMS messages while automatic import is enabled
  • detect whether a message is transaction-related
  • extract transaction details such as amount, sender, and transaction type
  • create transaction entries inside the app

How automatic import works

Everything below happens on your phone:

  • The first time you turn on automatic import, the app scans up to 50 recent inbox messages as a catch-up.
  • While it's on, the app checks every incoming SMS to see whether it's a transaction, including messages it then ignores.
  • Android may start a short background task when an SMS arrives, even if the app isn't open.
  • The app also checks for missed messages when it starts and when you return to it. If Android restricts or force-stops the app, it catches up when it can run again.
  • Matching messages go to the SMS review flow, and may be auto-posted under rules you set up.
  • Turning automatic import off stops processing new arrivals.

What is kept on your device

For transaction messages the app captures:

  • The sender and original message text are kept on your phone, including after you review, dismiss, import or auto-post them. Journal details can show the original SMS so you can check the entry against it.
  • New captures keep one copy in the shared device inbox; each workplace's records point to it rather than copying the text. Older records may still hold their own copy.
  • Imported entries also keep extracted details (amount, currency, merchant, reference number) and a one-way fingerprint used to avoid duplicates.
  • Privacy maintenance hashes older duplicate-check identities and strips raw SMS fields from automatic audit records. It does not erase kept source messages or notes you wrote.
  • New SMS imports never copy the raw message into journal notes, audit fields or analytics.
  • Kept SMS text has no automatic expiry. Importing, auto-posting, dismissing or turning off automatic import doesn't delete it. It stays until you clear app data, use Factory Reset, or uninstall the app.
  • Privacy Mode hides the original SMS text in journal details. SMS rules may keep the conditions you chose.

SMS review notifications

SMS review notifications are optional and scheduled locally on your device. Their enablement and detail-preview settings apply to this device and are not included in exported backups. By default they contain a generic review prompt. You can choose detailed previews with extracted transaction information; the app hides these details while Privacy Mode or app lock is enabled. Notification routing uses local inbox and workplace identifiers rather than raw SMS content. The pending inbox is shared on the device; posting, dismissing, and rule decisions belong to each workplace (a separate set of books in the app).

SMS permissions are optional, and the app remains usable without granting SMS access or enabling automatic import. Automatic import requires Android's SMS read and receive permissions. We do not intentionally upload full SMS content, sender details, or message bodies to external servers.

4. Voice Entry

You can optionally dictate a transaction instead of typing it. Voice entry asks for microphone and speech-recognition permission only when you start it. Speech is converted to text by your device's speech-recognition service, which, depending on your device and settings, may be provided by your phone's operating system vendor (for example Google on Android) and may process audio online under that provider's own privacy terms. The app then parses the resulting text on your device. We do not receive or store your audio recordings. Analytics may record that voice entry was started or that permission was denied, but not what you said.

5. Analytics and Crash Reporting

We use PostHog and Expo Insights for product analytics and technical improvement. Analytics may include only allowlisted event fields and values, including:

  • pages visited inside the app
  • onboarding completion
  • journal and budget creation (event counts only, not monetary values)
  • import and export actions (counts and formats, not row contents)
  • feature-usage events, such as starting voice entry
  • SMS import setting changes (enabled or disabled) and whether an SMS rule auto-posted a transaction (boolean values only)
  • in-app account creation events (account type and currency code only)
  • selected currency preference
  • app version, build, and variant
  • operating system name and version, and tablet flag
  • pseudonymous analytics identifiers
  • privacy policy acknowledgement events (policy version as event-specific data)

We do not intentionally send:

  • transaction or budget amounts
  • conversion or funnel monetary values
  • merchant names
  • SMS text
  • balances
  • user-entered notes
  • privacy policy text or signatures

Analytics and crash reporting are turned on in release builds of the app. There is currently no in-app setting to turn them off; if you object, contact us (section 15). Analytics identifiers are pseudonymous and are used only to understand app usage and improve functionality. PostHog session replay is disabled. We do not record in-app screenshots or screen replays for product analytics.

Sentry (crash reporting) is separate from PostHog. When enabled, error reports and performance traces may include error types, code locations (function names, file paths and line numbers from the app's own code), and navigation timing; error message text is removed before sending. Default personal data collection is turned off. Native crash reporting is disabled because its reports bypass the app's privacy filters, so native crashes, freezes and out-of-memory terminations are not reported. We do not attach screenshots or screen layouts, and we do not enable Sentry session replay or screen recording.

Bug reports you choose to send. If you use the in-app bug report option, the app builds a diagnostic ZIP (app and device details plus recent app logs) and opens your phone's share sheet. Nothing is sent unless you pick where to share it.

6. Internet Use

Although Full Frills Balance is primarily offline, internet access is used for limited features:

  • Exchange rates. Current rates are fetched from ExchangeRate-API. Historical rates for a past date are fetched from the open currency-api dataset (served via the jsDelivr CDN, with a Cloudflare-hosted fallback) or from Frankfurter's European Central Bank rates. These requests contain currency codes and, for historical rates, a date. They do not include your transaction history, amounts, or personal financial records.
  • Update check. Release builds may download a small public version-policy file from GitHub to tell you when an app update is available or required. The request does not include your financial data.
  • Analytics and crash reporting, as described in section 5.

Like any internet request, these services can see standard network information such as your IP address and the time of the request.

7. Export, Backup and Device Backup

You may export app data as ZIP backup files containing your personal records. Exported files remain under your control on your device or wherever you choose to save or share them.

Backups are not encrypted by the app, so you are responsible for protecting exported files. Backups you explicitly export may include original SMS content associated with the exported workplace records, including older journal source fields; the shared Device pending feed is not part of a workplace backup. Clearing app data does not delete backup files you previously saved or shared.

Android system backup. The app currently allows Android's built-in app-data backup. If you have device backup turned on (for example Google backup on Android), Android may include the app's local data in that backup under your Google account, and may restore it to a new device. This backup is controlled by Android and your Google account settings, not by us, and we cannot access it.

We do not sell personal data. Data may be processed by these parties, only for the purposes described:

  • PostHog and Expo: product analytics (section 5).
  • Sentry: crash reports and performance traces (section 5).
  • Your phone's speech-recognition service (for example Google on Android): audio when you use voice entry (section 4).

Files you export or bug reports you share go only where you choose to send them.

10. Permissions

Depending on features you choose to use, the app may request:

  • SMS read and receive permission, for SMS transaction import (Android)
  • microphone and speech-recognition permission, for voice entry
  • notification permission, for SMS review notifications
  • biometric or device-credential authentication, for the optional app lock
  • storage access on older Android versions (Android 12 and below), for export and backup files
  • internet access, for the features in section 6

Permissions are optional where possible and used only for app functionality. The app does not request access to your photos or videos.

11. Planned Future Online Features (Not Currently Available)

The current app does not offer account registration, online sync, or cloud backup. Future versions may offer optional encrypted backup or multi-device sync. If introduced, the intended privacy principles are:

  • sync requires a separate, clear opt-in after a notice explains what categories of data would be uploaded and why
  • declining sync keeps local use available
  • financial records are encrypted on your device before upload; the service is designed to store ciphertext without the keys needed to read the financial content
  • sync uses encrypted operations or snapshots rather than uploading the raw local database; balances, reports, and other derived views are rebuilt locally
  • sync is limited to the workplace you choose, but the service may still process account, device, membership, operation-size, timing, and security metadata needed to authenticate, authorize, route, and operate the service
  • recovery methods, device enrollment and revocation, key changes, retention, and deletion are explained before sync is activated
  • encryption and recovery keys are intended to remain under your control; losing your devices and recovery method may make synced books unrecoverable
  • raw SMS text is not uploaded or parsed on the server, and server-side tax, legal, or financial-reporting workflows are not part of the planned sync design

If online sync is introduced, this Privacy Policy and the in-app disclosure will be updated before the feature is enabled. Stopping sync would stop future uploads, but it would not automatically recall data already downloaded to a device or delete a server backup unless a separate deletion process is completed.

12. Security

Your financial records remain primarily on your own device. The app offers an optional app lock using your device's biometrics or credentials, and a Privacy Mode that hides amounts and SMS content on screen. You are responsible for securing your device and exported backup files.

14. Children's Privacy

Full Frills Balance is not directed to children. If you are below the age at which you can provide valid consent under the law that applies to you, use the app only with a parent or legal guardian involved.
